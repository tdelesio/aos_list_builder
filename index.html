AoS 4th Edition Battle Tactics Reference Codex

Take the Flanks

You complete this tactic if 2+ friendly units are wholly within 3" of opposing battlefield edges, and both are more than 9" from friendly territory.

Seize the Centre

You complete this tactic if 1+ friendly units are wholly within 6" of the battlefield's center, and no enemy units are within 6" of the center.

Slay the Entourage

Choose 1 enemy unit that was in the enemy General's regiment at start of game. You complete this tactic if that unit is destroyed this turn.

Take the Territory

You complete this tactic if you control 1+ objectives in enemy territory that were enemy-controlled at the start of the turn.

An Eye for an Eye

You complete this tactic if 1+ enemy units were destroyed this turn, and 1+ friendly units were also destroyed this turn.

Reclaim the Land

Choose 1 objective marker controlled by your opponent. You complete this tactic if you control that objective at the end of this turn.

AoS 4th Ed. Battle Setup Guide

1 Roll-Off for Roles

Both players roll off. The winner decides who is the Attacker and who is the Defender.

2 Pick Regiment Abilities

The Attacker selects their Regiment Ability, Enhancements, Spell Lores, Prayer Scriptures, and Manifestation Lores first. The Defender then selects theirs.

3 Choose Territory Layout

The Defender chooses which half of the battlefield they will use as their territory. The other half becomes the Attacker's territory.

4 Set Up Objectives and Terrain

Set up objective markers as indicated in your chosen Battleplan map. The Defender sets up standard terrain features first, followed by the Attacker.

A Deploy Faction Terrain

Players alternate setting up their faction-specific terrain features using their Deploy Faction Terrain abilities, starting with the player who finished setting up their army first (or decided by roll-off/battleplan).

B Deploy Regiments & Auxiliary Units

Players alternate deploying units onto the battlefield in entire Regiments or individual auxiliary units. Deploying an entire regiment counts as a single deployment action, greatly speeding up deployment for low-regiment armies!

C Deployment Phase Abilities

Use any other specific Deployment Phase abilities that are not placement abilities (such as vanguard moves, scouting, or reserves setups).

D First Turn Choice

The player who **finished deploying their army first** decides which player takes the first turn in Battle Round 1.

The Underdog Mechanic

At the start of Rounds 2, 3, 4, and 5, the player with the fewest Victory Points is designated the Underdog and receives +1 bonus Command Point (+5 total instead of +4).

Command Point (CP) Reset

Players receive 4 CP at the start of each round (5 if Underdog). However, all unspent CP are lost at the end of that battle round. No hoarding across rounds!

Round Priority Roll

At the start of Rounds 2-5, players roll off. The winner decides who takes the first turn.
Tiebreaker: If the priority roll is tied, the Underdog wins the tiebreaker. If VPs are also tied, the player who went first in the previous round wins the tiebreaker.

Double Turn Constraints

Choosing to go first in Round $N$ after going second in Round $N-1$ triggers a "double turn". In the official 4th Edition general battlepacks, taking a double turn means certain scoring elements or battle tactic completions may be restricted or provide bonus points to your opponent.
